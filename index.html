Erin — The Work

Erin Northcott

Erin Northcott is a Therapeutic Coach and the producer of “Daniel”, an HBO Original Documentary about her brother Daniel. Her work interconnects where story, body, and transformation meet. During the eleven years of carrying Daniel’s archive into an HBO documentary, her work expanded into Inner Ceremonies: a gentle, parts-work and Somatic practice for people moving through thresholds of their own.

Reel 02 — The Becoming

Inner Ceremonies

Inner Ceremonies are meditative, somatic sessions for deep self-connection.

Each session begins from the understanding that there is nothing to fix. Instead, there is tuning into the body, deep listening, realizations and a gentle honouring of your past. From there, new agreements can be made within yourself, that progress you toward what you want most.

Often there is a catalyst that sparks the desire to do this work: a life change, an important decision, a pattern you’ve started to notice, or a longing you haven’t yet dared to follow.

Sessions are one-to-one, body-led and unhurried. You don’t need to arrive with words.

Feel the profound relief of deepening your connection within.
