Thresholds

When life is asking you to rise

For some of us there is a numbness, like walls have been built around our hearts, and we may struggle to know what we really feel. Others can feel everyone else’s emotions but struggle with their own. Some of us feel something so strongly, and then negative thoughts, doubt, an obstacle or pattern is there again. We may know what we want, but then resistance pops up, and we become uncertain or even fearful. However it shows up, this internal unsteadiness can hold us back.

As we strengthen the “muscle” inside us that allows us to feel steady and self-assured, we open up to feeling and connecting with ourselves on a deeper plane. Our choices around relationships, finances, career, desires, and most often in who we choose in love; all shift as we gain internal steadiness.

Inner connection, honouring the past, moving through “obstacles”, and forming new inner agreements are at the heart of an Inner Ceremony. This gentle method can bring about real and lasting shifts for you.

In an inner ceremony, space is held for your inner world to explain itself. Like a raindrop ripples the pond, you’ll identify how it interconnects within many aspects of your life. Sometimes it takes a single session to identify something that has been running for a lifetime. Wherever you’re standing, the work meets you there, and you will feel so much lighter.

The Work

What happens in an Inner Ceremony

I love ceremonies, from the ceremonies I experienced in my years living in Bali, India and Taiwan, to experiences with plant medicine, and ceremonies around birth, life and death that I have been a part of. I know the power they hold when it comes to transformation and opening to what we truly want in life. I believe in the power and honour of holding a ceremonial space that feels grounded and safe enough for new neural pathways to form.

After each inner ceremony, we meet for an integration call. We will check in and see how the work you have done is feeling for you, and consider what other tools or exercises could serve you. We alternate in this way, one deeper session that lasts about 90 minutes, and one shorter one that is about 45 minutes. In this way we move through what you are wanting to work on, and we take time to make sure it is integrating into your life, in the way you desire.

The Session

Inside a session

Although we will talk, this is not talk therapy: together with tools like guided visualization and breath work, we travel past the thinking mind into a deeper, relaxed state, where your body holds the information you need. We find our way past the clutter of the mind, past the conditioning that life imparts on us, and into a more simple state of pure presence. The body is the opening, and from within this opening, tailored techniques like somatic experiencing, parts work, inner child work, and tailored exercises move you towards your desired outcome.

What no longer serves you can be honoured for how it once did, and new self-agreements can be made in a way that allows them to anchor, making the shifts that you desire in your life. These sessions are a path to knowing thyself and inviting in what you authentically want for yourself.

We work from the perspective that you already hold all the unique wisdom within you, and that your body already knows. That you are not broken, there is nothing wrong with you, nothing to fix. I am there to witness, hold and guide you to the clarity and results you desire.

An inner ceremony is medicine / substance free and done from the safety of your own home via a secure confidential zoom connection. The only thing you need there is your full presence.

You are always in control. You set the pace. Afterward, if appropriate, I create a personal integration recording: your own wisdom from the session, spoken back to you.

I wholeheartedly embrace all backgrounds, identities, genders, sexual orientations, cultures, religions, and relationship structures. Everyone is welcome here.

A note from me

“Uncovering Stuff”.

I know what it is to stand at a threshold and not know which way to move. I’ve been in the chair: for years, with practitioners of my own, doing the work of finding my way back. Very few of us have bloomed beyond childhood without some trauma, some conditioning, some limiting beliefs, or some fear of our own feelings and the very real power within us.

In my experience, when we dive in and risk “feeling it” or "uncovering stuff”, there may be a stunning awareness of seemingly unconnected events from our life coming together to answer questions, and offer us that “Ah-ha moment”.

The “it” or the “stuff” may feel unknown and like something to be feared, but with this “Ah-ha” we usually get a sense of wordless amazement, deliverance and relief. We then may feel how heavy the weight was that we had been carrying for so long. Tears of relief and exhaustion may leave us feeling calm, grateful and somber, in a good way.

Some of us grieve having not found this relief sooner, as we wonder what life would have looked like if we’d known what we do now. The comfort is, that this is “wisdom being formed”; by processing difficult questions and feelings that in turn, increase our intuitive understanding of what it is to be us.

I built this work out of what carried me.

If you’re standing somewhere unfamiliar, or all too familiar, right now — I have likely stood on similar ground. And I’d be honoured to walk it with you.
